# Featured project tiles (home page)

Scope: the art inside each featured-project card on the home page (`components/ProjectArt.tsx`), rendered again in the ePRO and DeID case-study heroes. Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: hiring managers, design leads, recruiters with little or no context. Action: open a case study.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each tile is one picture of the project's mechanism, with no authored text inside it, on a plum-family field with identical framing. The owner pinned a mix of image types chosen per story. It refuses the category default (a device mockup on a tinted field) and the typographic title card.

OWN-WORLD: The site's incumbent world: plum-family fields tinted per tile, flat cream shapes at hairline weights, rose for the single accent in each picture, real product screens where the story is the product itself. No gradients, glows, glass, label strips, or statements.

STORY: A reader with no context sees what each project does in one glance (speech becoming an answer, a scan series being redacted under review, a withdrawal moving through screens, one component scaling into a system), and clicks to learn how.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two-column grid of 3:2 tiles. ePRO: an SVG waveform resolving into five option pills with the third filled rose. DeID: an SVG series of eighteen frames, eleven with identifiers blacked out, the twelfth outlined rose for review. Cashier: three real withdrawal screens layered at depth, the amount screen in front, cropped by the tile. Design systems: one SVG card component at three scales on a column grid, its button rose. A supplied photograph or external image, when set on a project, replaces the built-in picture full-bleed with the same framing. The primary action stays the card's "View case study" link.

FORM: Pictures per project (code-drawn illustration, staged real screens, or a supplied image), the owner's choice after rejecting text-led tiles. Seed key 9f9586e6, surface scope, experience mode, degraded roll (no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

# Featured project tiles (home page)

Scope: the art inside each featured-project card on the home page (`components/ProjectArt.tsx`), rendered again in the ePRO and DeID case-study heroes. Visitor mode: Experience. Audience: hiring managers, design leads, recruiters with little or no context. Action: open a case study.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each tile is a field note from the project: one instrument line at the top with a single position marked, one statement in the serif, and a source and state line beneath, on a plum-family field. The grammar is identical across the four; only the instrument, the words, and the tint change. It refuses the category default (a device mockup on a tinted field) and the photo-collage opposite.

OWN-WORLD: The site's incumbent world: plum-family fields tinted per tile, cream type with secondary text tinted from cream, Spectral for the statement, Inter small caps for the instrument and meta lines, hairline rules, a rose outline only on the DICOM redaction bars. No photos, screenshots, gradients, glows, glass, or label strips.

STORY: A reader with no context gets the project's tension in one sentence and one instrument (a patient journey, a redaction tag row, a withdrawal state track, a system's layers), understands what the project is, and clicks to learn how it was resolved.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two-column grid of 3:2 tiles. ePRO: journey ruler 01-05 with 04 Missed entry marked; the sticky's words; source and mood. DeID: PatientName, PatientID, StudyDate with values blacked out; "One study. Close to 100,000 images. Every one of them redacted by hand before a regulator could see it."; before and after lines. Cashier: track 01-03 with 02 Pending marked; "Where is my money right now?"; source and state. Design systems: layers 01-04 with 02 Components marked; "Two systems that let teams outside design prototype on their own."; madhaus.io, 2026; In progress. The primary action stays the card's "View case study" link.

FORM: Field notes, the structure the owner approved on the ePRO tile, applied to all four after rejecting photo and screenshot material on the others. Seed key 9f9586e6, surface scope, experience mode, degraded roll (no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
